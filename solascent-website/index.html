05 — Product care · Make every scent last

Quality in Every Detail.

A few simple steps can help you get the most out of your SOLASCENT diffuser and enjoy its fragrance throughout your space.

01

Set it up

Remove the stopper and insert the reeds into the fragrance bottle. Allow them to absorb the fragrance for 5–6 hours.

02

Let the scent flow

Place your diffuser in a suitable area where air can circulate naturally, allowing the fragrance to disperse throughout your space.

03

Refresh your fragrance

Turn the reeds periodically to refresh the fragrance and maintain its presence.

04

Handle with care

Keep away from direct sunlight, heat, and flames. Avoid contact with skin and eyes, and keep out of reach of children and pets. Do not ingest.
